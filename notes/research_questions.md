# KRAS Research Questions

## Main Question

How are KRAS mutations distributed in pancreatic adenocarcinoma?

## Why am I studying this?

- I want to understand KRAS mutations in pancreatic cancer.
- I want to learn how cancer genomic data can be analyzed using bioinformatics.
- I want to identify common KRAS protein changes in the dataset.
- I want to understand the relationship between DNA changes and protein changes.

## Dataset

- Source: cBioPortal
- Study: Pancreatic Adenocarcinoma (TCGA, PanCancer Atlas)
- Study ID: `paad_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018`
- Gene: KRAS

## Things I want to find

- How many unique samples contain KRAS mutations?
- Which KRAS protein changes are present?
- Which protein changes occur most frequently?
- Which KRAS positions are commonly mutated?
- What are the corresponding DNA-level changes?
- Are different KRAS mutations associated with different copy-number states?
- What biological information can be learned from these mutations?

## Questions I don't know yet

- Which KRAS mutation is most common at the sample level?
- How many unique samples contain G12D?
- How many unique samples contain G12V?
- How many unique samples contain G12R?
- What percentage of samples contain each mutation?
- What patterns can be identified from the data?

## Current Stage

Dataset exploration and initial computational analysis.
